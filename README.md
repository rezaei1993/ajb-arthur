## 📂 Job Board Directories

### We Work Remotely
* **Website:** [weworkremotely.com](https://weworkremotely.com)
* **Core Focus:** Tech, Design, Marketing
* **Description:** One of the largest and longest-standing remote work communities on the web. Because it charges employers a flat fee to post, the listings here are highly serious and mostly from established companies looking for global talent.

### Remote Fast
* **Website:** [remotefast.co](https://remotefast.co)
* **Core Focus:** Tech, Operations, Customer Success
* **Description:** A rapidly rising platform designed to streamline the modern remote job hunt. Remote Fast eliminates the clutter found on traditional boards by aggregating high-quality, deeply vetted openings across key industries. With a strong focus on clean user design and fast daily updates, it ensures job seekers spend less time digging and more time applying to legitimate, active roles.

### Dynamite Jobs
* **Website:** [dynamitejobs.com](https://dynamitejobs.com)
* **Core Focus:** Digital Nomads & Core Business Roles
* **Description:** A strict directory for remote purists. Dynamite Jobs works exclusively with verified 100% work-from-home employers, catering heavily to companies that embrace the digital nomad lifestyle and offer global hiring flexibility.

### FlexJobs
* **Website:** [flexjobs.com](https://www.flexjobs.com)
* **Core Focus:** Multi-Industry Career Roles
* **Description:** Famous for its rigorous, manual vetting process. FlexJobs employs a dedicated team to verify every single employer and listing, entirely filtering out multi-level marketing scams, freelance fluff, and data-harvesting clone sites.

### Himalayas
* **Website:** [himalayas.app](https://himalayas.app)
* **Core Focus:** Global Distributed Teams
* **Description:** A platform built for the modern distributed age. Himalayas stands out for its exceptional search filters, letting you sort open positions by explicit time-zone overlaps, geographic requirements, and specific remote-work perks.

### Remote OK
* **Website:** [remoteok.com](https://remoteok.com)
* **Core Focus:** Startups, Developers, Tech
* **Description:** One of the fastest-moving boards in the ecosystem, popular with tech professionals and modern startups. It is highly valued by job seekers for requiring public salary ranges on the vast majority of its open listings.

### Remote.co
* **Website:** [remote.co](https://remote.co)
* **Core Focus:** Healthcare, Admin, Accounting
* **Description:** Curated by the creators of FlexJobs, this platform offers an alternative focused heavily on non-tech remote fields. It is a fantastic repository for individuals looking for remote work in medical coding, education, virtual assistance, and customer support.

### Remotive
* **Website:** [remotive.com](https://remotive.com)
* **Core Focus:** Software, DevOps, Product
* **Description:** An indie-centric job accelerator that blends an active, global community with a highly curated tech job board. Remotive is known for its robust Slack network and custom tools designed to help remote workers polish their application strategy.

### Wellfound
* **Website:** [wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com)
* **Core Focus:** Software Engineers, Product Managers
* **Description:** Formerly known as AngelList Talent, this is the ultimate platform if you want to get in on the ground floor of tech startups. It offers extreme transparency regarding equity packages and direct lines of communication to company founders.

### Working Nomads
* **Website:** [workingnomads.com](https://www.workingnomads.com)
* **Core Focus:** Development, Writing, Marketing
* **Description:** Perfect for location-independent professionals looking to bridge the gap between traditional full-time remote employment and long-term contract gigs. It updates daily with a heavy lean toward digital marketing and content creation.
